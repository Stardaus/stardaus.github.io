# T2DM Herbal & Dietary Supplement Study

Co-authored cross-sectional clinical research studying herbal and dietary supplement consumption patterns among Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) patients in rural Selangor, Malaysia.

## Citation & Publication

- **Journal**: *Malaysian Journal of Pharmacy* (2025), 11(1).
- **Authors**: Wan, A. G. T., Hing, N. Y. L., Koh, K. Y., Choong, K. E., Hassim, H., **Mustar, M. F.**, Kamal, N. M., & Nasir, N. M.
- **Key Insight**: Evaluated prevalence, patient motivations, and clinical interactions of complementary supplement usage to inform evidence-based counseling in primary care health clinics.
