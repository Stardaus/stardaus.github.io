# District Medication Formulary PWA

Built as a mobile-first, offline-accessible Progressive Web App (PWA) using **React** and **Vite** to digitize district formulary data and regional antimicrobial treatment guidelines into a high-performance, searchable local cache.

## Key Highlights

- **Instant Searchability**: Sub-millisecond response times for searching drug dosing, local formulary availability, and clinical indications.
- **Offline First**: Utilizes Service Worker caching so primary care clinicians can access crucial guidelines even without internet connectivity in rural clinics.
- **Workflow Improvement**: Streamlines drug reference checks during busy outpatient dispensing sessions at Klinik Kesihatan.
